Privacy policy

Last updated: 4 October 2026.

Pino Drive Sync uses authorized Google Drive file contents and metadata solely to synchronize files and support recovery from synchronization changes.

File copies, recovery copies, and operational records may be stored in locations controlled by the account holder. Authorization credentials are retained to support unattended synchronization.

The application does not sell Google user data, use it for advertising, or disclose it to unrelated third parties. Google processes data according to its own policies. Existing sharing permissions continue to apply to files stored in Google Drive.

The account holder controls retention and deletion of files, recovery copies, operational records, and authorization credentials. Access can be revoked through Google Account settings. Revocation stops future authorized access but does not remove existing copies.

For questions, use the support contact shown on the application's Google consent screen.
